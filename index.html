Time Moves On

A time bending 2D roguelike platformer where you can find new friends, but eventually, will have to depart with them.

Scroll

Twelve friends will join your journey.

Each friend grants an ability. They can leave, but if you reconnect, their ability will be leveled up!

01

Hohenberg · The Upper Plain

At the top
lies Brunn

Vertical platforming Alpine Difficulty: medium

The Alpine islands of Hohenberg, rise above the kingdom. Wooden scaffolding and light fabrics sway in the wind. Right angles dominate, making you use wall bounces, upward parries and high jumps. Missing a platform is punishing, and gravity works against you.

School of Brunn. Home of the royal family Antritz and the most prestigious school in the kingdom, despite its current state.

02

Wellenfeld · The Middle Plain

Keep the flow
to reach Frun

Horizontal momentum Grassy Hills Difficulty: easy

The grassy field of Wellenfeld, strechts across islands of rolling hills. Mounds, trees and tall grass sway gently. Shallow angles allow Kudo to preserve momentum, creating a rhythm of traversing gaps.

School of Frun. Speed and horizontal momentum, the most widely practiced tradition in the kingdom.

03

Tropffall · The Lower Plain

Down at bottom
Saus awaits.

Controlled falling Night · bioluminescent Difficulty: hard

Tropffall is the most dissolved terrain in the kingdom. Tear-shaped islands, form the landscape. Descend through long drops, manage fall speed, and dive along a teardrop's curve to convert a fall into horizontal speed.

School of Saus. What others consider dangerous, Saus students consider a resource.
